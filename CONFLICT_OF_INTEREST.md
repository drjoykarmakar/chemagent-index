# Conflict of interest

All three included DemoChem systems are maintained with this platform and are openly disclosed as software fixtures. Their algorithms and deterministic error injection are visible in source. They are not independent AI competitors.

For any future evaluation of a maintainer-developed model, agent or company product:

1. Disclose ownership, employment, financial interests and authorship alongside the run.
2. Use identical benchmark versions, tool budgets, scoring, execution and publication rules.
3. Preregister the evaluation configuration and preserve versions and run artifacts.
4. Have independent reviewers oversee manual judgments and disputes; authors recuse themselves.
5. Make the evaluation independently reproducible, subject to legitimate dataset restrictions.
6. Never adjust category weights or suppress failed tasks to favor the system.

A reviewer cannot certify their own system. Funders and commercial users receive no control over public ranking outcomes.
