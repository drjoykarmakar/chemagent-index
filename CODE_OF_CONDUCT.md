# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors, reviewers and users respectfully. Welcome people across disciplines and experience levels. Critique methods and evidence, not identities. Do not harass, discriminate, disclose private information or retaliate against good-faith reports.

Maintain honest scientific records. Do not fabricate results, misrepresent uncertainty, manipulate rankings or conceal conflicts of interest. Respect dataset licenses and privacy.

Maintainers may moderate content, warn contributors, restrict participation or remove accounts in proportion to documented behavior. Affected maintainers should recuse themselves from decisions. Sensitive allegations and personal information must remain outside public issues. Maintainers are responsible for establishing a private reporting channel.
